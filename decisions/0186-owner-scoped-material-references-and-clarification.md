# 0186: Owner-scoped material references and clarification

Status: Implemented bounded slice in clean-platform source; verified locally;
not deployed by this change.

Descriptive values cannot independently serve as aliases for their containing
object. Identity-bearing properties and declared identity frames retain that role.
Material facts do not imply a count. A later reference to constituents creates
an owner-scoped uncounted collection using existing graph primitives, and later
facts attach to that group rather than the owner. Explicit language data supplies
number forms; morphology never merges physical identities or unrelated owners.
Exact material facts produced by generated Paths use the same primitive.

Ambiguous references keep one device-local, revision/version-bound continuation:
original assertion, at most eight exact candidate identities, source relation
witnesses, ten-minute expiry and three unsuccessful replies. Sentence Paths parse
owner fragments and possessives. A reply narrows those candidates and replays the
whole transaction, or leaves the graph unchanged. Cancellation, stale evidence,
expiry and a new complete ordinary request discard the continuation. Publication
remaps candidate IDs without putting the continuation in shared graph facts.
Protected boundaries and action authority do not change.

Clean-platform decision 0134 records contracts and regression/browser evidence.
Unknown material inflections still need lexical evidence, and historical
misattributed owner facts are not automatically rewritten. This is not general
ontology inference or a measured 95% success rate. Unproved generated programs
are never activated/shared merely because they produce a clarification.
