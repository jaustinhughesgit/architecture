# 0182: Staged solar dismissal and compact overview

Status: Implemented bounded slice.

Inspector outside clicks have three ephemeral stages: dim all interior dots to
30% while preserving size, shrink dots and hide lines, then close the open system.
An inside click during either reversible stage restores previous expansions.
Panning does not count; explicit navigation or a new ordinary response spotlight
exits the stages. The camera and filters are not reset.

Closure honors the existing managed-workspace leave guard and leaves no active
managed workspace. Already-admitted owner/promoted-owner circles use nearest-first
hexagonal packing about the frozen visible world center, clearing temporary
center overrides only on overview entry. Existing finite motion animates collapse
and convergence; reduced-motion users receive the same final state immediately.
Reopening retains exact identity, current authorization and existing solar motion.
Subsequent drags remain ephemeral and cannot overlap other closed summaries.

This extends the solar projection, not discovery, graph persistence, authority or
protected execution. Marketplace candidates remain in the open system's outer
orbit until closure. See product decision 0130 and solar layout/browser tests.
